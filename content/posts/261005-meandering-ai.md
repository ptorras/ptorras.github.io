So, first things first. Why did I even feel the need to write this? It is highly likely that all of this text just ends as fodder to some bigger and better LLM anyway [^1].


Well, here's the annoying truth. Regardless of how much I dislike the motivations or the ethos of those developing this technology and regardless of how meaningless they make me feel as a computer scientist, these things _are_ useful. It has come to the point where defending the position of not using AI at all is just self-sabotage because everybody else is. And let's face it, I am neither working on a niche domain where AI is still unreliable nor I am one of those code alchemists that hold together the logical fabric that holds our computers' most intricate pieces of software [^2]. I am just another guy stumbling along , learning as much as I can along the way and so are many others. Pretending that we can somehow compete against this technology fairly is like pretending that a farmer can compete with an army of tractors.

Or at least, pretending that a farmer can compete with an army of tractors _while there's still gas left_.
